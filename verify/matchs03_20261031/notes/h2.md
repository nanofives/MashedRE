`*` means the real size, which is larger than Ghidra's (551 B). The g2s scores are the converter's ops %. Per the coordinator I skipped 0x00415220, 0x00416a30, 0x00417da0, 0x00416250 and 0x00412f30, which h4 now owns.

Findings worth carrying:
- **`.rdata` constants:** Ghidra's `_DAT_005cxxxx` float constants are usually compiler literals (`__real@` pool). With an extern, cl loads the constant first (`fld K / fmul [mem]`). With the literal it emits `fld [mem] / fmul K`. Write them as literals. Exception: cl factors `a*0 + b + c*0` into `(a+c)*0 + b` for literals and externs alike, so a sum of zero-products needs parentheses (0x00415e20).
- **fsubr pattern:** the original's `fld [b] / fsubr [a]` for `a - b` (the pilot's (a) candidate) comes from `d.x = b.x; d.x = a.x - d.x;`. It moved 0x00412e30 from 85 to 93, and 0x00412cf0 and 0x00413cb0 use it as well. This weakens the pilot's (a) classification for that residue.
- **Store order of constants:** for runs of constant stores (1.0/0.0), the register choice (eax vs ecx zero) follows the statement order. Brute-forcing the permutations works (24 or 120 compiles).
- **0x00413cb0 group file:** the real caller of 0x00413bc0 is in this file, and 0x00413bc0 stays byte-identical with it (ESI = j, ECX = uv).
