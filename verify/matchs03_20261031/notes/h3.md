Finding that applies elsewhere: the 0x005cXXXX float operands are compiler literals (0x005cc94c = 2^32,
the unsigned->float fixup). Writing them as literals instead of extern floats fixes the fmul/fadd operand
order (member loaded first, then `fmul [const]`). This took 0x0041af50 from 83.0 to 88.8. 0x005d757c (0.0f)
is read from memory, so keep it as an extern. A negative literal (-0.125f) makes 13.00.9176 fold the sign
into fsubr, which the original does not do.
