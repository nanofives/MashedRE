## 3. Findings outside the gates (report only)

- **The stores are live from round 2.** Pre-store `0x0089a368` = 1 in rounds 2 and 4, and `0x0089a370`
  is non-zero in every later round. So the per-round re-arm of the band ladder and the one-shot roll,
  predicted in PREREG §1.3, happens. In round 1 both are 0, as predicted.
- **Seed vs chain.** P414 and P414_CD3 (round-1 cd 3,1,4,2 vs 3,3,3,3) are identical until step frame
  1986, and they diverge only when round 2's cd differ. So `VehiclePhysicsRun.cpp`'s one-shot seed
  overwrites the chain's round-1 arm (as RESULT_LEG1B §3 described for the bog). Rounds 2+ get their
  boost only from the chain.
- **(b) depends on ring phase.** P414_CD3 differs from L1B_M5CD only by the body's 4 draws in round 1
  (its stores are no-ops there). The step dumps diverge at frame 59, and `ai_ctrl_window.py --check`
  goes from 1 PASS + 2 FAIL (5 items) to 3 FAIL (10 items). P414 scores the same as P414_CD3 in the
  window (`P414_B_SCORE.txt`). A (b) verdict therefore depends on how many ring draws precede the
  window, which the port cannot match: the original's call index is unknown (U-9198, AiStandalone.cpp
  ring comment).
- **bias374 reversals:** 2 (P414) and 1 (P414_CD3), against 0 in every earlier arm and in CTRL. This
  is the first non-zero reading of symptom (a). Cause not isolated.
- **cd vs the original:** the port draws a spread (1100..1400), and the original measured 3,3,3,3
  (1300) on one capture. U-9198 is filed. One capture cannot tell a fixed ring position from chance.
- D2 WATCH: first differing ptrace line at index 0 in both arms, the same pre-existing f=1 speed digit
  as every leg-1b arm. Not new.
