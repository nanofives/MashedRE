Observations (13.00.9176/9178 vs retail 13.00.9466):
- 4 of 6 are byte-exact with 9176/9178 (5 of 6 if 0x00475ef0 is scored at its real 57-B size). Nothing in the integer, control-flow, frame-layout, stack-slot-packing, constant-in-register (ebx=2, edi=22050.0f bits) or prologue-sinking codegen needed a different compiler.
- Both residuals are x87-only and both look like instruction-selection/scheduling around one x87 op: 0x00414f00 picks `fsubr` with the eax-based operand loaded first (only for the components whose base register is reloaded or freed next, not the middle one); 0x0045e990 hoists independent integer loads/stores between `fsub` and `fstp`. No source change moved either. If 9466 has a different x87 scheduler/operand-order heuristic, these two are the specimens to test it on.
- The integer-only blocks of 0x0045e990 (six constant-position blocks, ~300 instructions) match exactly, so the scheduler differs at most around x87 instructions.
- Tool note: `re/console/pc_funcs.csv` gives 0x00475ef0 size 49, real body is 57 B (ends at `ret` 0x00475f28, NOP fill from 0x00475f29). match.py has no size override.
- No `in_*` / `unaff_*` register inputs and no non-standard conventions in any of the six (all cdecl), so nothing here points to /GL.
