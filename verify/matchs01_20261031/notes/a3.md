Observations
- The `DAT_005cc3xx/5cc5xx/5cc7xx/5cc8xx/5cc9xx` and `DAT_005d757c` operands are .rdata compiler literals (0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 60, 0.625 ...). Write them as `0.5f` etc., not externs. As externs 9176 swaps fld/fadd operand order and never folds them (0x004041c0: 95.2% as externs, 100% as literals). 0x005cc970 is a double holding (double)57.29578f.
- Without /Op, 9176 reassociates float sums (`y + 3.0f + 60.0f` becomes y + 63) and factors common factors (`x*z*P + x` becomes x*(z*P+1)). Explicit parentheses `(y + 3.0f) + 60.0f` and the `x + (z*x)*P` shape block this.
- Frame-size misses from lone float locals: the y values in 0x004041c0 and 0x00403fa0 are the .y of 8-byte {x, y} locals (RwV2d). A lone float gives a frame 4 or 8 bytes short.
- Byte-wise RwRGBA colours: the order of the colour assignment statements (and their position relative to the float statements) picks the al/cl/dl byte registers. 0x00403fa0 needed a brute force over 120 statement orders (3 hit 100%).
- Structs: 0x00636b88 Entry[6] (0x14 B, float at +0x10). 0x00404fa0 Anim {+4 count, +0xc duration, +0x10 Key*}, Key 0x24 B {+4 time, +8 RtQuat, +0x18 RwV3d}. DAT_00639d70/74/78 = {Key-anim ptr, float time, clump ptr} state used by 0x004053d0..0x00405540.
