## 3. Standalone gate ring

`mashedmod/src/mashed_re/D3d9Render/TrackRenderer.cpp:2166-2255`: it scans the track `.piz` for the **COURSE.LUA-declared gate BSP** (`gate_bsp_`, else the `AI*.BSP` name heuristic, `:2174-2177`), parses it with `Track::World::Parse` (`:2182`), sizes `gates_` from `aw.materials.size()` (`:2183`), takes **gate index = material RED byte** (`:2197`), averages the quad verts into `center` (`:2202-2216`) and derives `c0/c3` + `dir` (quad normal oriented toward the next gate) at `:2218-2250`; the ribbon is then handed to `RaceCamera` as `cam_nodes_` (`:2257-2269`).
