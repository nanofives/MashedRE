## Signature

## Purpose
Draws a column of up to 4 rows. Each row is a left "Arrow" quad, a glyph (0x59 or 0x5a, chosen by whether
a per-index DWORD in the 0x007f1058 table is non-zero) at x = 350.0, and a right "Arrow" quad placed after the
glyph's measured width. Row y starts at the value FUN_0042ac50 returns for the count FUN_0042ac00 takes from a
per-screen descriptor list, and steps by 30.0. The colour is black with alpha taken from the low byte of
DAT_0067e814, quartered when the per-screen row flag is 0. The whole draw is skipped when DAT_0067e810 == 2,
or when DAT_0067e810 == 0 and the DWORD at DAT_0067e814 is below 0x60. The meaning of the per-index DWORD,
the 4 entries and the mode values is not established (U-9659).
