## Correction (negctl re-earn, 2026-10-12)

Read from the bytes of `MASHED.exe.unpatched`; see `re/analysis/negctl/reearn_20261011/fix_settings_RESULT.md`.

- __cdecl with TWO stack args (HWND, id): the HWND is read from `[esp+0x108]` (`0x00499778`) and the id from `[esp+0x10c]` (`0x00499752`) after `sub esp,0x104`. Callers push id then HWND and clean 8 (`0x00498f71..0x00498f84`). It does NOT take the HWND in EAX.
