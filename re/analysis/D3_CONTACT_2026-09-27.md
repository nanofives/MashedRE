### 2.2 The points-vs-vectors finding

Re-running the archived C4 lane reproduced GREEN
(`log/diff_rw_matrix_rotate.csv` 10/10, `log/diff_rw_v3d_transform_points.csv` 7/7), but
the second is **GREEN by construction** — both sides dispatch the same device method, so
any argument order passes. Decoding its 7 archived rows shows the vectors were degenerate:
every row reproduces `out = mat[0] * in-read-as-the-matrix's-right-row` (`_TRANS`
pos=(1,2,3), in=(1,1,1) → `(1,1,1)`, translation never applied; `_MIXED` in=(-1,2,-3) →
`(-2,4,-6)`), i.e. the device method read its matrix out of the zeroed tail of the
64-byte `in` buffer.

New arg_type `device_transform_points` (`re/frida/diff_template.js`, additive; the old
handler is untouched so its archived verdict is not silently redefined) calls
`fn(out, in, 1, mat)` and zeroes all three 64-byte buffers. Four registry entries, 10
vectors each, including two Z-rotations and one rotation with pos = (1.5, -2.5, 0.75):

| hook | original | reimpl side | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rw_v3d_transform_points_argfix` | `0x004c3df0` | `RwV3dTransformPoints` (the ported thunk) | **GREEN 10/10** — `log/diff_rw_v3d_transform_points_argfix.csv` |
| `rw_v3d_transform_points_cpu` | `0x004c3df0` | `RwV3dTransformPointsCPU_C` | **RED 4/10** — `log/diff_rw_v3d_transform_points_cpu.csv` |
| `rw_device_dispatch_0c_points` | `0x004c3d90` | `RwV3dTransformPointsCPU_C` | **9/10, the 10th off by 1 ULP** — `log/diff_rw_device_dispatch_0c_points.csv` |
| `rw_v3d_transform_vectors_cpu` | `0x004c3df0` | `RwV3dTransformVectorsCPU_C` (new) | **GREEN 10/10** — `log/diff_rw_v3d_transform_vectors_cpu.csv` |

The 4 RED rows are **exactly** the 4 whose matrix has a nonzero translation row:
`_TRANS` in=(1,1,1) original `(1,1,1)` vs port `(2,3,4)`; `_MIXED` in=(1,1,1) original
`(15,18,21)` vs port `(26,30,34)`; `_MIXED` in=(-1,2,-3); `_ROTA`. The other 6 (pos = 0)
are bit-identical.

Two explanations survived that on its own: slot +0x14 is the vectors method, or the
method branches on the RwMatrix flags word `m[3]` (0 in these vectors). The third row
discriminates them: the **same** points impl, the **same** inputs, the **same** `m[3] = 0`,
against the **other** dispatcher, comes out 9/10 with a 1-ULP summation difference on the
10th (`3227474774` vs `3227474773`). Same flags, different verdict ⇒ the slot decides.

**Conclusion (resolves U-1891):** slot **+0x14** = transform **vectors**; slot **+0xc** =
transform **points**. `RwV3dTransformVectorsCPU` (new, `Math/RwV3dTransformPointsCPU.cpp`)
is the correct stand-in for `FUN_004c3df0` and is bit-identical to the original on all 10
vectors, including the 4 that discriminate. `Collision::Rw_TransformPoints` binds to it.

### 5.1 P_MINE diverges, with the mechanism cited

The mechanism, disassembled:

- `FUN_00457ef0` (P_MINE FIRE) runs its body only on fire mode 2:
  `MOV ESI,[EAX+0xac]` `0x00457ef5` (the ARM handle), `CMP [ESP+0xc],2` `0x00457efb`,
  `JNE 0x00457f21` `0x00457f00` (return). On mode 2 it calls `0x00457c10` with the

  `0x00457caa` — and `CALL 0x0045c110` `0x00457cf4`; `TEST EAX,EAX` `0x00457cfc`;
  **`JNE 0x00457e08`** `0x00457cfe`.
- `0x00457e08` is the epilogue: `POP ESI; ADD ESP,0xb4; RET` (`0x00457e08..0x00457e0f`).
  So a refused gate leaves **no** state change.
- The `+0x08` decrement sits after both gates and after the placement call
