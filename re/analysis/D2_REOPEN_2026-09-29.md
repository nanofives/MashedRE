## 3. THE PASS RULE (pre-registered)

### 3a. Per-metric bound

For each of the three metrics, the port's scored solo value must satisfy

> `|port - mean_orig|  <=  tol`,   `tol = max( 3h , 2% of mean_orig )`

`3h` covers the whole observed original-side spread with margin. The 2% floor is the tolerance
this project already uses for D2-class comparisons (`re/analysis/U9138_FIX_2026-09-28.md` §5
driving-median "within 2%"; `re/tools/ai_speed_env.py:115` `BAND_PCT = 2.0`) and stops the
`2000-2600` band — where the original happens to be almost perfectly repeatable — from being
given an absurdly tight 0.18% band.

| metric | mean_orig | 3h | 2% of mean | **tol (the larger)** | **PASS interval** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| slip 1500-2000 | 0.19245 | 0.00390 | 0.00385 | **±0.00390** | **0.18855 .. 0.19635** |
| slip 2000-2600 | 0.249875 | 0.00045 | 0.00500 | **±0.00500** | **0.24488 .. 0.25487** |
| driving-median | 1943.57 | 30.02 | 38.87 | **±38.87** | **1904.70 .. 1982.44** |

### 3b. Port-side determinism precondition

U-9148 records that one HEAD solo run in five lands in a second attractor
(`1818.47` vs `1505.12` driving-median). So a single port run may not be scored.

> Take **at least 3** port solo runs. At least 3 of them must agree with each other within the
> ORIGINAL's own half-range `h` on every metric (±0.0013, ±0.00015, ±10.005). The agreed value
> is the scored value. Every non-agreeing run is reported with its numbers. If no 3 runs agree,
> the arm **FAILS on determinism** regardless of where the numbers land — an arm that cannot
> reproduce itself cannot be compared to a deterministic reference.

### 3c. Verdict

D2 re-closes only if **all three** metrics fall inside their §3a interval **and** §3b is
satisfied. Two-of-three is a FAIL, and is reported as a FAIL.

### 3d. What is NOT a gate here (reported, not scored)

Per the session directive, and to keep this rule from being gamed:

- criterion (e) (`re/tools/ai_speed_env.py --check`), AI criterion (b)
  (`re/tools/ai_ctrl_window.py --check`), the power-up sweep
  (`re/tools/pu_replay/sweep.ps1`) and the modes oracle are **regression guards**. They are run
  after each fix and **reported as-is**. If a fix that is faithful to the original moves one of
  them, that is stated plainly and the fix is kept — the guards are not tuned back.
- The ONE hard non-metric gate is the build: `mashedmod\build.bat` must complete with the
  dual-copy guard reporting **`NEW=0`** (`scripts/lint_rva_bodies.py:120-122`). No fix may
  introduce a new duplicate body.

**Answer: `[esp+0x3c]` is `E+0x0c`, A4's `param_3` STACK SLOT, which A4 reuses as a
local at `0x004706a2` to hold `record + [record+0x9a8]*0x40 + 0x928`.** A6b's ESI is
therefore *literally the same pointer* A5 gets as its second argument
(`0x00470918`) and A6a as its third (`0x00470928`). Reading the slot as
"param_3/input" or as "param_4/xform" both mis-identify it, which is how the tree
came to hold two different wrong answers at once.

**The frame is fully accounted for.** U-9149 recorded "`add esp, 0x24` does not
balance A6a's four pushes plus A6b's two, so there is a `sub esp` or further pushes
unaccounted for." There is neither: the `0x24` cleans **A5's three** as well
(3+4+2 = 9 dwords = 0x24), and the five prologue pushes are undone by the five
epilogue pops. `push ecx` at `0x00470670` is the MSVC 4-byte-local idiom, used at
`0x004707b7`/`0x004707bb` for the `fild` int->float staging. **U-9149's missing
evidence is closed.**

### 4.2 The object is an RwMatrix

From A6b (`py -3.12 re/tools/disasm_fn.py 0x00468980 0x00468b40`, `RET`s at
`0x00468aba` / `0x00468b34`):

- `0x00468a31`, `0x00468aaf`, `0x00468b29` all `call 0x4c4d20` = `RwMatrixRotate`,
  with `push esi` as the **matrix** argument (`0x00468a30`, `0x00468aae`, `0x00468b20`).
- `0x004689b8` `fld [esi+0x24]`, `0x004689c1` `[esi+0x20]`, `0x004689cc` `[esi+0x28]`
  dotted with `0x006146fc`; `0x00468a36` `[esi+4]`, `0x00468a48` `[esi]`,
  `0x00468a4c` `[esi+8]` with the same. Read from the binary,
  `0x006146f0 = (1,0,0)`, `0x006146fc = (0,1,0)`, `0x00614708 = (0,0,1)`.
  So those are `at.y` (`[esi+0x24]`, word index 9) and `right.y` (`[esi+4]`, word
  index 1) of an `RwMatrix` — matching `AeroStabilize.cpp`'s `orient[9]` / `orient[1]`.

`0x40` is `sizeof(RwMatrix)`, which is the same stride A3's lead-2 fix corrected to.

### 4.3 A second correction that fell out: which argument A5 consumes

`VehicleControl.cpp` carried "the original passes A4's param_4 (the xform) here, NOT
iVar1/wheelBlock". A balanced ESP walk through A5 (`0x0046ddb0` to its `RET` at
`0x0046e9d0`, delta 0) finds A5's references into its caller's frame are:

| slot | meaning | reads |
|---|---|---|
| `E5+0x04` | dt | 6 — `0x0046df2a` `0x0046e2dc` `0x0046e3d1` `0x0046e3e0` `0x0046e3eb` `0x0046e92a` |
| `E5+0x08` | the matrix | 2 — `0x0046ddb0`, `0x0046de01`; **both feed the matrix argument of `RwV3dTransformVectors`** (`0x0046ddc9`, `0x0046de1f`) |
| `E5+0x0c` | A4's param_4 | **0** |

So A5's transform matrix is the `+0x928` RwMatrix and **A4's param_4 is dead inside
A5**. The same walk on A6a (`0x00467650` to its `RET` at `0x0046888e`, delta 0) gives
`E+0x04` (car) 1 read, `E+0x08` (dt) 8, `E+0x10` (input) 4, and **`E+0x0c`
(the matrix) 0 reads** — A6a is handed the pointer and ignores it.

**So the original's A-chain has exactly TWO consumers of the `+0x928` matrix: A5 and
A6b.** That removes A6a from the U-9147 candidate list (the port hands A6a the
contact-ring pointer where the original hands it the orientation matrix, and it
cannot matter, because A6a never reads that argument).
