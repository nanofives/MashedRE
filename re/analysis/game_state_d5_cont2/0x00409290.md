## Purpose
`FUN_00409290` snaps the int at `*p` into one of the ranges of the LAPDATA `Safe_Start_Lines` table. A value below a range's low end becomes that low end. A value above every range becomes the first range's low end. A value inside a range is left alone.
- The table is the course-block field the Lua binding `Safe_Start_Lines` (0x0047a810, `re/analysis/c2gen/track/0047a810.md`) fills: u16 pairs at `[0x0066d658 + i*4]` (low) and `[0x0066d65a + i*4]` (high), count `[0x0066d6e0]` (`mashedmod/src/mashed_re/Race/PathNodes.cpp:14`, `:83-89`, byte-compared against the original's live block, gate G-P1).
- Its one live use in the round flow: `FUN_0040e590` passes `&DAT_0063ba80` at the round-start placement (`re/analysis/bucket_util_0040e4b0_0042f790/0x0040e590.md:49`). `DAT_0063ba80` is the node the next round grids at (`verify/d3_bulk_20261011/roundgrid/FIX_BRIEF.md` section 2: written `__ftol(progress[survivor])` by `FUN_00410860` at 0x004108c5, read by `FUN_00408b00`).
