## Correction 2026-10-12 (U-9671)

The "vtable[0x48]" reading above is wrong. Bytes of `original/MASHED.exe.unpatched` (capstone; the Ghidra pool decomp
agrees, `(**(code **)(in_EAX[3] + 0x48))(in_EAX[3])`):

0x0041c2d1 8bf0      mov  esi,eax
0x0041c2d3 8b460c    mov  eax,[esi+0xc]
0x0041c2d6 50        push eax
0x0041c2d7 ff5048    call [eax+0x48]
0x0041c2da 8b4604    mov  eax,[esi+4]
0x0041c2dd 50        push eax
0x0041c2de ff5048    call [eax+0x48]

0x0041c2f1 5e        pop  esi
0x0041c2f2 c3        ret

- 35 bytes (0x0041c2d0..0x0041c2f2 inclusive), not 34.
- For each member m = block[3], block[1], block[0], block[2] (block arrives in EAX): `push m; call [m+0x48]`. The
  call target is the dword at m+0x48, one dereference. There is no `mov reg,[m]` before the call, so it is a
  function-pointer field of the member, not a vtable slot.
- The member calls are caller-cleaned: one `add esp,0x10` at 0x0041c2ee for all four pushes.
- ESI is saved and restored (0x0041c2d0 / 0x0041c2f1). EAX at `ret` is the last member call's EAX.
- Callers by Ghidra function boundary (`decomp_pc.py --callers`): 0x0041a3e0 (tail jmp 0x0041a3ee, EAX = 0x0063c610)
  and 0x0041c300 (tail jmp 0x0041c30e, EAX = 0x0063cda4). Callees: none direct (four indirect calls).
