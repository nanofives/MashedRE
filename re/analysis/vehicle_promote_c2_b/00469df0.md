## Purpose

Vehicle-vehicle convex-hull SAT contact test and impulse response. Two-pass test against both vehicles' 4-vertex bounding hulls (12 floats at +0xA28..0xA54), computes contact centroid, applies coefficient-of-restitution impulse to both vehicles' velocities, adds angular impulse, enqueues debug event type=4.
